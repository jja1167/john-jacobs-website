About Me

Hi, I'm John! I'm an aspiring full-stack software developer, and a recent graduate.

I am a recent graduate with a Bachelor’s Degree in Software Engineering from Western Governor’s University. I also have an Associates degree from Sinclair Community College. I am proficient in object-oriented programming languages like: Java, C++, and Python. I am also proficient in web based languages such as HTML5 and CSS3, as well as database languages such as MySQL and PostgreSQL. I am also familiar with Agile software development methodologies, like Scrum, and have experience working in a team environment.

I am also certified in CompTIA Project+ and am an AWS certified Cloud Practitioner.

I first discovered computer programming in high school when I took a Python course, and I have been passionate about it ever since. One of the thing I really like about programming is the ability to create something from nothing, and the satisfaction of seeing your code come to life and satisfy some user need.
