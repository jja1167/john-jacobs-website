Interests

I have a variety of interests that I enjoy doing or researching in my free time.

One of my biggest interests is cars. I enjoy learning about different makes and models, as well as how they work. The Ram TRX is my favorite, but I also enjoy Japanese (especially JDM), American, European, and classic cars. I also like big offroad vehicles, like the Ram TRX or Ford Raptor.
Another interest of mine is aviation. I enjoy learning about different types of aircraft, as well as how they work. I also enjoy watching airshows and learning about the history of aviation. My favorite aircraft is the Lockheed AC-130 gunship, but I also enjoy learning about other aircraft, such as the Messerschmitt Bf-109, F-22 Raptor, and F-35 Lightning II.
I also have an interest in heavy machinery, such as construction equipment and agricultural machinery. I grew up in the country, so I have always seen and been around heavy machinery, like tractors, combines, and semi trucks. One of my favorite pieces of heavy machinery is the combine harvester, as it is a very complex machine that can do a lot of work in a short amount of time. I also like how all the different parts of a combine work together to create one impressive machine.
I also enjoy playing video games in my free time. I enjoy playing a variety of different types of games, such as action games, racing games, and simulation games. Some of my favorite video games are the Farming Simulator series, Forza Horizon series, the Mudrunner series, and super-hero games like Marvel's Spider-Man series, and the Batman Arkham series.
I also enjoy hiking and spending time outside. I also enjoy outdoor activities, such as archery, biking, and kayaking.
